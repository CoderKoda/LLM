Maksym Ivanovych Mudryi (Ukrainian: Максим Іванович Мудрий; born 5 May 2003) is a Ukrainian professional footballer who plays as a central midfielder for Ukrainian First League club Nyva Ternopil.


Career


Lviv
He made his professional debut for Lviv in the losing Ukrainian Cup match against Vorskla Poltava on 30 September 2020.
